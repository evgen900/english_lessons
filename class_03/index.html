📚 Past Simple — The Basics

We use Past Simple for actions that are finished in the past.

✅

Regular Verbs

Add -ed to the verb

play → played walk → walked watch → watched
⚡

Irregular Verbs

These change completely — must memorise!

go → went eat → ate see → saw
❌

Negative

Use didn't + base form

He didn't go She didn't eat
❓

Questions

Use Did + subject + base form

Did he go? Did she eat?
